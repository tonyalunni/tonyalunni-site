Why act

Upkeep consumes 40% of the team's capacity

In the example, a 5-analyst team spends two of every five hours keeping 80 reports running, before anyone builds anything new.

The approach

Six steps, from mapping the work to keeping it clean

STEP 1 · SET UP

Agree decision rights

A one-page charter: scope in and out, success measures, and who approves. Business leaders approve every retirement; the reporting lead recommends.

STEP 2 · MAP THE WORK

Find where time waits

Requests in the example take 13 business days, but only 5 are work. One intake form and a 2-day acknowledgment target cut the waiting, not the build.

STEP 3 · LISTEN AND MEASURE

Pair voice with usage

A questionnaire with a 2-week deadline, then follow-ups with leaders who did not reply and owners of reports no one named.

STEP 4 · DECIDE

Use explicit rules

Keep, merge or retire each report by the same rules, with owner sign-off for anything critical and safeguards on every retirement.

STEP 5 · REBUILD

One entry point

A catalog organized by who uses each report, one template, defined metrics and a named owner on every report.

STEP 6 · SUSTAIN

Gate what comes in

Every new request passes a catalog check. A quarterly usage review flags reports for the owner to confirm or retire.

Decision rules

Rules for speed, owner sign-off for anything critical

Retire · 32

Under 3 named users in 90 days, no scheduled distribution, and the owner confirms no control, regulatory or period-end use.

Review with owner

Low usage, but distributed on a schedule or critical. Never retired without the owner's sign-off.

Merge · 34 into 8

Overlaps another report's core metrics and audience. Fold into one owned report.

Keep · 14

Widely used, distinct and owned. Move to the standard template.

Example judgment call. A regional leader relies on a weekly regional view that duplicates the director report. Resolution: keep the regional cut as a filtered page inside the director report, approved by that leader and the report owner.

Safeguards on every retirement: 2 weeks' notice to users, a 60-day archive, owner sign-off.
